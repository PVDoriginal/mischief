tldr; fuck "AI". 

This acts both as a policy and a standard for the community. 

When I say "AI", you know what I mean. Whether you call it LLMs, or agentic coding, or content generation. I don't like saying it without the 
quotes because it feels like an insult to AI as a field. But I'm sure you know exactly the sort of thing I'm referring to. It hurts 
writing this document because I don't want to bring more attention to this "AI". 

**Mischief does not, and will never, accept "AI" contributions**. 

I could list a hundred reasons for why I find it unethical, gross, sloppy, greedy, lazy. But I won't. Because reasons shouldn't matter. Reasons can be debated, but this document
shouldn't be debatable. 

None of this is based on political context, circumstances, laws, copyright issues, alleged accusations.  

I don't care for technicalities or results, about "better" or about quality of output. I don't care if it's "the future", or if everyone on the planet besides me is using it. 
"AI" could do the work of 100 developers in a second, better than them, for free, with no ecological effects. That wouldn't change anything written here. 

Programming is my craft, my artistic expression, and Mischief is a monument to that.

Anyone who considers "vibe-coding" to be anything but slop is not welcome here. Anyone who wants to debate this document is not welcome here. If you are bothered by what is written here, 
you are not welcome here. 

**How is this Enforceable?**

It's not. It can't be. That's a very weird thing to say. No rule is fully enforceable. The point of this document isn't to act as some token of purity, but to set 
a standard and a goal for the community and potential contributors. To be a barrier for people who we do not want to interact with. 

If a good liar and deceiver sneaks in, that is a whole different issue that has nothing to do with this document. 

**The Consequences**

Anyone who tries to debate this policy, who tries to suggest using "AI"-generated code or who insists on adding "MCP" and similar "AI"-based 
tools into the engine, who tries to find a loophole or otherwise circumvent the policy, will be banned. 

We also reserve the right to ban any individual who acts as if advertising "AI" products (also knows as a "booster" in some circles) or who talks about "AI" even after they have 
been prompted not to. This includes showcasing content that you are aware was significantly (or less significantly) generated by "AI", whether it was visible art or code.   

**What About Rubber-Ducking?** 

Using a chat-bot as part of trying to think of a solution to a problem is generally allowed. We don't recommend or encourage it, and may actively frown upon it 
(especially if you've paid money for it!) but it doesn't directly go against this policy. As long as the "AI" was used purely as a search engine and 
didn't generate the solution for you, think for you, or otherwise acted as a relevant actor in your choices and thinking, you *might* be welcomed here. 

Still, this is an "AI-Sober" space. So please try to not copy "AI" output into conversations or encourage others to seek it for help. We would like to 
have a community where we can code creatively and passionately without mention of a product that we are so burnt out and tired of.
